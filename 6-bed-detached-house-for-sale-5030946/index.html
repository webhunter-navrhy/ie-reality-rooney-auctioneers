Description

Rooney Auctioneers present to the market this large six bedroomed detached home, situated on a site of approx. 0.5 acres with a detached single-storey garage and double height.

This property provides an excellent opportunity for an owner to establish a large home just a short distance from Limerick City Centre.

Internally, the ground floor boasts three living rooms, a study, large kitchen, utility room, guest W.C. and an ensuite bedroom.

Upstairs are five double bedrooms, the main having an ensuite bathroom, followed by a main bathroom.
